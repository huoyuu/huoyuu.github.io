My hair grows, turns yellow, and falls out. Does that make me a tree?

我的头发会生长、泛黄、掉落，那我也是树吗？

My thoughts travel far, colorless and formless. Does that make me the wind?

我的思绪也会远行、无色、无形，那我也是风吗？

My soul floats, wanders, and dissolves. Does that make me a cloud?

我的灵魂也会漂浮、游荡、消散，那我也是云吗？

My love shines, dims, and flickers. Does that make me a star?

我的爱意也会闪耀、暗淡、明灭，那我也是星吗？

My body also twists, wriggles, and rolls around—so am I a maggot too?

我的身体也会扭曲、蠕动、翻滚，那我也是区吗？
